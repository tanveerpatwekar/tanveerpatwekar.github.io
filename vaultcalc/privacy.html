VaultCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

VaultCalc is an EMI, SIP, FD and GST calculator for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to pocketfold.apps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The numbers you type and the calculations you save stay on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), you are asked for consent first.
  • We use Google Firebase to count how the app is used and to receive crash reports. These never include the amounts you enter.
  • The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Information that stays on your phone

Your calculator inputs, your saved History and your settings (theme and language) are stored only on your device. We do not have a server that receives them. You can delete saved calculations in the History screen, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include your History and settings in your Google account backup, according to your device's backup settings, so they come back on a new phone. You can turn this off in your phone's settings. Your ad-consent choices are not backed up: on a new phone you are asked again.

When you share a result or export a PDF, the image or file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

VaultCalc shows banner, native and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app version and language),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • how you interact with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, VaultCalc uses Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to ask for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the app. Elsewhere, you can limit personalised ads or reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

App usage and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Firebase Analytics tells us how the app is used, for example which calculator was opened, whether a result was shared or exported as a PDF, and which language was chosen. It records events, not your numbers: no amounts, rates or other inputs are ever sent. Analytics uses an app instance ID and receives device information such as model, operating system, app version and approximate region. Analytics follows the consent choices you make in the consent form described above.

Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits a serious error, so that we can fix it. A report contains technical details such as the error's stack trace, the screen that was open, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust some settings in the app (such as the size of the ad banner) without an update. It uses a Firebase installation identifier and does not involve your personal data.

See Privacy and Security in Firebase for details.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. VaultCalc only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can hide the ads and restore the purchase on another device signed in to the same Google account. We never see your card or payment details.

Ratings

After you have used the app a few times, it may ask Google Play to show its standard "rate this app" prompt. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with anyone other than the Google services described above, which process it to provide ads, analytics, crash reports and purchases.

Children

VaultCalc is a finance tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

Information sent to Google's services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy & ad choices (where available).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Delete your saved calculations in History, or clear the app's storage.
  • To ask about, or request deletion of, data linked to your app installation, write to pocketfold.apps@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
